# Week 4 Training Plan — 26 October–1 November 2026

**Master goal:** 1:30:00 half marathon on 21 March 2027  
**Phase:** Aerobic rebuild + threshold development  
**Week objective:** Reach a routine 90–100 min aerobic long run, consolidate longer threshold work, freshen slightly late in the week, and use Saturday as the next all-out 5K benchmark.

## Intensity anchors

- **Easy / aerobic HR:** ~132–145 bpm
- **Long-run HR:** ~135–145 bpm
- **Threshold HR expectation:** ~160–170 bpm
- **Recovery HR:** ~120–138 bpm
- **5K benchmark:** pace-led, with HR mainly used for post-run validation

---

## Monday 26 October — Long aerobic run

**Total:** 90–100 min

| Segment | Duration | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm-up | 10 min | 6:15–6:45/km | 125–138 bpm |
| Main aerobic | 70–80 min | 5:45–6:15/km | 135–145 bpm |
| Cool-down | 10 min | 6:20–6:50+/km | <135 bpm |

Keep the run aerobic throughout. Short hill rises are fine, but avoid sustained HR above ~148 bpm.

---

## Tuesday 27 October — Reduced strength / no run

**Duration:** 30–35 min  
**Effort:** RPE ~6/10.

Use the same core movements as previous weeks but reduce total volume slightly so Wednesday and Saturday remain high quality.

---

## Wednesday 28 October — Threshold 2 × 15 min

**Warm-up:** 15 min at **5:50–6:25/km**, HR **130–145 bpm**, then 3–4 relaxed strides.

**Main set:** **2 × 15 min at 4:43–4:53/km**, with **4 min easy jog** recovery.

| Segment | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rep 1 | 4:43–4:53/km | ~159–166 bpm |
| Rep 2 | 4:43–4:53/km | ~163–170 bpm |
| Recovery | 6:15–7:00/km | Aim to fall toward <150 bpm |

**Cool-down:** 10–15 min easy.

The second 15-minute rep should remain controlled. Do not turn the final five minutes into a time trial.

---

## Thursday 29 October — Easy aerobic + strides

**Easy run:** 40 min at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR **132–145 bpm**.

Then **4 × 20 sec strides** at approximately **3:50–4:10/km**, with 60–90 sec easy recovery.

Keep this session light enough to improve Saturday freshness.

---

## Friday 30 October — Rest

Full rest or light walking only.

---

## Saturday 31 October — 5K benchmark / parkrun

**Purpose:** Test progression after four weeks of controlled aerobic and threshold work.

### Warm-up

- 2.5–3 km at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR roughly **135–150 bpm**
- 3–4 × 20 sec strides at **3:50–4:10/km**
- 2–4 min easy before the start

### Race target

**Target range:** **22:00–22:10**  
**Average pace:** approximately **4:24–4:26/km**

| Section | Pace / effort | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| km 1 | ~4:27/km; controlled | HR lag; likely ~168–180 bpm by end |
| km 2 | 4:24–4:26/km | ~181–188 bpm |
| km 3 | 4:24–4:26/km | ~184–190 bpm |
| km 4 | Hold 5K effort on hills rather than forcing exact pace | ~187–194 bpm |
| km 5 | Race hard if form and breathing remain good | ~190–200+ bpm possible late |

Do not target a particular maximum HR. The goal is an evenly distributed hard effort and the strongest possible final kilometre.

---

## Sunday 1 November — Recovery

**30–45 min** at **6:20–7:00/km**, HR **120–138 bpm**, or 40–50 min very easy cycling/walking.

---

## Week-success criteria

1. 90–100 min aerobic running feels routine rather than draining.
2. 30 min of threshold work is completed with little pace fade.
3. Thursday and Friday leave the legs fresh for Saturday.
4. A 5K around 22:00–22:10 would keep the early-December 21:20 projection broadly alive, but pacing quality and physiological control matter more than forcing the forecast.
