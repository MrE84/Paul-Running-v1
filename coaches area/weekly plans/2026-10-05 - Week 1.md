# Week 1 Training Plan — 5–11 October 2026

**Master goal:** 1:30:00 half marathon on 21 March 2027  
**Current 5K benchmark:** 22:47 (3 October 2026)  
**Phase:** Aerobic rebuild + threshold development  
**Week objective:** Absorb the 3 October parkrun, restore aerobic volume, begin controlled threshold work, retain speed, and make Saturday a controlled 5K rather than another PB attempt.

## Intensity anchors

- **Easy / aerobic HR:** ~132–145 bpm
- **Long-run HR:** ~135–145 bpm; short hill rises to ~148 bpm are acceptable
- **Threshold HR expectation:** ~158–168 bpm, possibly touching ~170 bpm late in the final rep
- **Recovery HR:** ~120–138 bpm
- Pace is secondary on easy running. On threshold work, breathing and control override pace if the physiological cost is wrong.
- HR during strides and the first kilometre of a 5K will lag; do not use HR as the primary real-time target there.

---

## Monday 5 October — Long aerobic run

**Purpose:** Aerobic durability and recovery from Saturday's hard 5K.

**Total:** 80 min

| Segment | Duration | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm-up | 10 min | 6:20–6:50/km | 125–138 bpm |
| Main aerobic | 60 min | 5:50–6:20/km | 135–145 bpm |
| Cool-down | 10 min | 6:20–6:50+/km | <135 bpm |

**Execution:** Keep this genuinely aerobic. Do not chase pace on hills. If HR is persistently >148 bpm at the planned pace, slow down.

---

## Tuesday 6 October — Strength / no run

**Purpose:** Running economy, lower-body strength and injury resistance.

**Duration:** 35–45 min  
**Effort:** RPE 6–7/10; no grinding reps.

Suggested work:
- Bulgarian split squats
- Romanian deadlifts
- calf raises
- glute work
- core
- single-leg stability

Keep enough reserve that Wednesday's threshold session is not compromised.

---

## Wednesday 7 October — Threshold 3 × 8 min

**Purpose:** Move LT2 pace forward while keeping the effort controlled.

**Warm-up:** 15 min at 5:55–6:30/km, HR ~130–145 bpm, then 3–4 × 20 sec relaxed strides.

**Main set:** **3 × 8 min at 4:50–5:00/km** with **3 min easy jog** between reps.

| Segment | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rep 1 | 4:50–5:00/km | ~155–163 bpm |
| Rep 2 | 4:50–5:00/km | ~160–166 bpm |
| Rep 3 | 4:50–5:00/km | ~162–168 bpm; brief ~170 acceptable late |
| Recoveries | 6:15–7:00/km | Aim to fall toward <150 bpm |

**Cool-down:** 10–15 min easy, >6:10/km, HR trending below ~140 bpm.

**Control rule:** The final rep should be hard but controlled, not 5K breathing. If breathing becomes ragged early, reduce pace by 5–10 sec/km.

---

## Thursday 8 October — Easy aerobic + strides

**Purpose:** Add low-fatigue aerobic volume and maintain neuromuscular speed.

**Easy run:** 40 min at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR **132–145 bpm**.

Then **4 × 20 sec strides**:
- Pace reference: **3:55–4:15/km**
- 60–90 sec easy jog/walk recovery
- Smooth acceleration; no sprinting
- HR is not a useful target because the reps are too short for HR to catch up

---

## Friday 9 October — Rest

No structured training. Walking and normal daily movement are fine.

---

## Saturday 10 October — Controlled parkrun

**Purpose:** Practise disciplined pacing and running economy without another all-out PB effort.

### Warm-up

- 2–3 km easy at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR roughly **135–150 bpm**
- 3–4 × 20 sec strides at **3:55–4:15/km** with full easy recovery
- Finish the final stride 2–4 min before the start

### Parkrun

**Target:** **4:40–4:45/km average**  
**Expected finish:** approximately **23:20–23:45**, course dependent.

| Section | Pace / effort | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| km 1 | ~4:45/km; deliberately restrained | HR lag; likely ~165–178 bpm by end |
| km 2–3 | 4:40–4:45/km | ~175–182 bpm |
| km 4 | Hold effort on hills; do not force watch pace | ~178–185 bpm |
| km 5 | Maintain control; only press late if breathing is good | ~180–188 bpm |

**Key rule:** Do not repeat the 4:01 opening kilometre from 3 October. The goal is to finish feeling that another gear was available.

---

## Sunday 11 October — Recovery

Choose one:

### Option A — Recovery run
- 30–40 min
- Pace: **6:20–7:00/km**
- HR: **120–138 bpm**

### Option B — Low-impact recovery
- 40–50 min very easy bike / walk
- Conversational effort

Choose Option B if legs are sore or Saturday created more fatigue than expected.

## Week-success criteria

The week is successful if:
1. Monday stays genuinely aerobic.
2. Wednesday produces 24 min of controlled threshold work without respiratory distress.
3. Saturday begins conservatively and does not become an accidental PB race.
4. Recovery is good enough to start Week 2 without residual soreness or unusual fatigue.
