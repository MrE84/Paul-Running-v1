# Week 2 Training Plan — 12–18 October 2026

**Master goal:** 1:30:00 half marathon on 21 March 2027  
**Phase:** Aerobic rebuild + threshold development  
**Week objective:** Extend threshold duration, maintain aerobic volume, and use Saturday as the next all-out 5K benchmark.

## Intensity anchors

- **Easy / aerobic HR:** ~132–145 bpm
- **Long-run HR:** ~135–145 bpm
- **Threshold HR expectation:** ~158–169 bpm
- **Recovery HR:** ~120–138 bpm
- **5K benchmark:** pace-led, with HR used mainly for post-run validation because of HR lag and terrain

---

## Monday 12 October — Long aerobic run

**Total:** 80–85 min

| Segment | Duration | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm-up | 10 min | 6:15–6:45/km | 125–138 bpm |
| Main aerobic | 60–65 min | 5:50–6:20/km | 135–145 bpm |
| Cool-down | 10 min | 6:20–6:50+/km | <135 bpm |

Keep the run smooth and conversational. Short hill rises above 145 bpm are acceptable, but avoid sustained work above ~148 bpm.

---

## Tuesday 13 October — Strength / no run

**Duration:** 35–45 min  
**Effort:** RPE 6–7/10.

Focus on:
- Bulgarian split squats
- Romanian deadlifts
- calf raises
- glutes
- core
- single-leg stability

Do not train to failure.

---

## Wednesday 14 October — Threshold 3 × 10 min

**Warm-up:** 15 min at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR **130–145 bpm**, then 3–4 relaxed strides.

**Main set:** **3 × 10 min at 4:47–4:57/km**, with **3 min easy jog** recovery.

| Segment | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rep 1 | 4:47–4:57/km | ~156–163 bpm |
| Rep 2 | 4:47–4:57/km | ~160–167 bpm |
| Rep 3 | 4:47–4:57/km | ~163–169 bpm |
| Recoveries | 6:15–7:00/km | Aim to fall toward <150 bpm |

**Cool-down:** 10–15 min very easy.

The target is 30 min of controlled threshold work. If HR rises above ~170 bpm early or breathing becomes 5K-like, slow down.

---

## Thursday 15 October — Easy aerobic + strides

**Easy run:** 40–45 min at **5:50–6:20/km**, HR **132–145 bpm**.

Then **5 × 20 sec strides** at approximately **3:50–4:10/km**, with 60–90 sec easy recovery.

Keep the strides relaxed and technically clean.

---

## Friday 16 October — Rest

Full rest or light walking only.

---

## Saturday 17 October — 5K benchmark / parkrun

**Purpose:** Test whether the current improvement trend is continuing.

### Warm-up

- 2.5–3 km at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR roughly **135–150 bpm**
- 3–4 × 20 sec strides at **3:50–4:10/km**
- 2–4 min easy before the start

### Race target

**Target range:** **22:20–22:30**  
**Average pace:** approximately **4:28–4:30/km**

| Section | Pace / effort | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| km 1 | 4:30–4:32/km | HR lag; likely ~165–178 bpm by end |
| km 2 | 4:28–4:30/km | ~180–186 bpm |
| km 3 | 4:28–4:30/km | ~183–189 bpm |
| km 4 | Hold effort on hills rather than forcing pace | ~185–193 bpm |
| km 5 | Race; increase only if form and breathing permit | ~188–200+ bpm possible late |

**Primary pacing rule:** Do not chase the 20-minute group from the gun. The first kilometre must feel restrained.

---

## Sunday 18 October — Recovery

**30–40 min recovery run** at **6:20–7:00/km**, HR **120–138 bpm**, or substitute 40–50 min very easy cycling/walking if legs are sore.

---

## Week-success criteria

1. 30 min of threshold work is completed with controlled breathing.
2. Thursday leaves the legs fresher, not more tired.
3. Saturday is paced evenly enough that the final kilometre is one of the strongest.
4. A result around 22:20–22:30 would keep the early-December 21:20 projection broadly on trend, but execution matters more than forcing the forecast.
