# Week 3 Training Plan — 19–25 October 2026

**Master goal:** 1:30:00 half marathon on 21 March 2027  
**Phase:** Aerobic rebuild + threshold development  
**Week objective:** Increase aerobic durability, extend controlled threshold work to 36 minutes, and use Saturday as a progression session rather than another maximal 5K race.

## Intensity anchors

- **Easy / aerobic HR:** ~132–145 bpm
- **Long-run HR:** ~135–145 bpm
- **Steady finish HR:** ~148–158 bpm
- **Threshold HR expectation:** ~158–170 bpm
- **Recovery HR:** ~120–138 bpm

---

## Monday 19 October — Long aerobic run + optional steady finish

**Total:** 85–90 min

| Segment | Duration | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm-up | 10 min | 6:15–6:45/km | 125–138 bpm |
| Main aerobic | 65–70 min | 5:45–6:15/km | 135–145 bpm |
| Optional steady finish | final 10 min | 5:15–5:30/km | ~148–158 bpm |
| Cool-down if needed | 5 min | very easy | <140 bpm |

Only use the steady finish if legs feel good and HR drift is modest. Otherwise keep the whole run easy.

---

## Tuesday 20 October — Strength / no run

**Duration:** 35–45 min  
**Effort:** RPE 6–7/10.

Suggested work:
- Bulgarian split squats
- Romanian deadlifts
- calf raises
- glute work
- core
- single-leg stability

Keep 2–3 reps in reserve on the main lifts.

---

## Wednesday 21 October — Threshold 3 × 12 min

**Warm-up:** 15 min at **5:50–6:25/km**, HR **130–145 bpm**, then 3–4 relaxed strides.

**Main set:** **3 × 12 min at 4:45–4:55/km**, with **3 min easy jog** recovery.

| Segment | Pace | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rep 1 | 4:45–4:55/km | ~157–164 bpm |
| Rep 2 | 4:45–4:55/km | ~161–168 bpm |
| Rep 3 | 4:45–4:55/km | ~164–170 bpm |
| Recoveries | 6:15–7:00/km | Aim to fall toward <150 bpm |

**Cool-down:** 10–15 min very easy.

The goal is 36 min of quality work with no major pace fade. If rep 3 becomes a survival effort, reduce pace rather than forcing the session.

---

## Thursday 22 October — Easy aerobic + strides

**Easy run:** 45 min at **5:50–6:20/km**, HR **132–145 bpm**.

Then **5–6 × 20 sec strides** at approximately **3:50–4:10/km**, with 60–90 sec easy recovery.

---

## Friday 23 October — Rest

No structured training.

---

## Saturday 24 October — Progression parkrun

**Purpose:** Build speed endurance without racing from the gun.

### Warm-up

- 2–2.5 km at **5:55–6:25/km**, HR roughly **135–150 bpm**
- 3 relaxed strides

### 5K progression

| Section | Pace / effort | Expected HR |
|---|---:|---:|
| km 1 | 4:55–5:00/km | ~160–170 bpm by end |
| km 2 | 4:50–4:55/km | ~168–175 bpm |
| km 3 | 4:45–4:50/km | ~172–180 bpm |
| km 4 | 4:35–4:45/km effort-adjusted for hills | ~178–187 bpm |
| km 5 | 4:20–4:30/km if still controlled | ~184–195 bpm |

Treat the hills by effort, not by forcing the watch to display the target pace. The final kilometre should be the fastest if execution is correct.

---

## Sunday 25 October — Recovery

**30–45 min** at **6:20–7:00/km**, HR **120–138 bpm**, or 40–50 min very easy cycling/walking.

---

## Week-success criteria

1. The long run reaches 85–90 min without excessive cardiac drift.
2. Threshold volume reaches 36 min with stable form and controlled breathing.
3. Saturday is genuinely progressive rather than a disguised all-out 5K.
4. The week finishes with enough freshness to attempt the Week 4 benchmark.
